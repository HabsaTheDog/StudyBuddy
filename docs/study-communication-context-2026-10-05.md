## Reported behavior and change

A natural request to apologize to a course teacher for delayed participation
produced an unresearched draft without the recipient or affected session.
The native communication owner now researches these fields through deterministic
configured-source tools before authoring the draft. It uses observed names,
roles, addresses, dates and times, and asks a targeted question when the sources
cannot resolve the recipient or appointment. It preserves late participation.

Direct page results retain explicit mailto contacts with page provenance; calendar
events retain organizer/attendee metadata without assuming teaching roles.
Portal HTTP credentials are bound to the configured origin. New source inventory
and calendar operations keep private calendar URLs inside the broker. Separate
`scope:"today"` selects the whole local day for implied current delays, including
already-started sessions, while retaining the original request. Unknown course
matches remain explicitly labeled evidence candidates.

Mail account policy applies to addressed drafts even without an explicit mail
noun. Reading bodies requires a positive existing-message request; contact
lookup, templates, drafts, negated reads and future subordinate send references
do not imply mailbox access or permission to send. Observed sender names and
addresses remain together. No subject, institution or person rule was added.
