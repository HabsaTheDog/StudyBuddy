# Music brief

## Creative target

- Emotional arc: curious and slightly overwhelmed → focused momentum → capable and optimistic.
- Mood: intelligent, kinetic, modern, precise, uplifting.
- Energy: immediate pulse, controlled build, one clear lift for the integration reveal, and a resolved button ending.
- Approximate tempo: 105–135 BPM.
- Suitable palette: electronic percussion, tactile clicks, warm synth bass, restrained arpeggios, clean tonal impacts.
- Avoid: corporate ukulele, sentimental piano, aggressive trailer brass, busy vocals, comic effects, or generic EDM festival drops.

## Edit requirements

- One continuous 45–65 second passage with a musically complete beginning and ending.
- Strong opening within the first two seconds for the prompt constellation.
- A phrase boundary or lift suitable for the Moodle/CIS/Calendar/Mail activation.
- Enough rhythmic detail to support rapid output transformations without overwhelming readable UI.
- Prefer a natural button ending or resolved cadence; a clean authored 60-second edit is ideal.

## Usage and licensing

- Primary delivery: website/YouTube in 16:9.
- Adaptation: TikTok/Reels in 9:16.
- Organic and paid social advertising use should be permitted.
- Worldwide online distribution for StudyBuddy promotional use.
- Free-first search with a soft ceiling of EUR 15 per track.
- Prefer no attribution and no Content ID claims.
- Instrumental only.
