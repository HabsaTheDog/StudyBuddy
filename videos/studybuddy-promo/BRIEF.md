## Intent

Create a short, polished teaser for StudyBuddy aimed at university students. The
film begins with several familiar student prompts appearing independently, then
shows them being sent into StudyBuddy. StudyBuddy reaches across Moodle, CIS,
Calendar, and Mail to gather the academic context a generic chat assistant does
not already have, then transforms that context into useful study outputs.

The tone is energetic, intelligent, and cinematic rather than tutorial-like.
Music, sound design, kinetic typography, and readable product UI carry the story
without a conventional voice-over.

## Assets

- Existing StudyBuddy logo and brand assets in the repository — closing lockup and visual identity.
- Current StudyBuddy interface — source of truth for recognizable product UI.

## Customizations

- Opening prompt constellation: examples include exam preparation, weekly deadlines, course questions, and drafting an email to a lecturer.
- Connected sources shown as active product features: Moodle, CIS, Calendar, and Mail.
- Output montage: direct answers, generated study PDFs, interactive study guides, practice quizzes, and Moodle Quiz support.
- Primary 16:9 master at 1920x1080 plus a purpose-built 9:16 adaptation at 1080x1920.
- Live storyboard and wireframe review before full animation.
- Pace the film to the approved 31-second edit of “Technology Future Innovation Pulse”: prompts establish the problem before the first drop; the final quiet pocket becomes a clean logo slate and fade-out.

## Notes

- Do not label Mail as coming soon.
- Position StudyBuddy through demonstrated student-specific context rather than an unsupported blanket claim that it is better than every other AI.
- Keep on-screen copy concise enough to read at normal playback speed.
