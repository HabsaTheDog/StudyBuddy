# Study Buddy Corporate Identity

## Core Colors

| Role | Bright | Dark | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary blue | `#323a61` | `#19254b` | Main brand color, app chrome, primary actions, headings, document structure. |
| Highlight gold | `#dfbb63` | `#c3994d` | Highlights, objects of interest, focus states, key separators, important callouts. |

## Application Rules

- Blue is the primary brand color. Use it for navigation, primary controls, titles, document headers, and durable UI structure.
- Gold is an accent, not the base theme. Use it to draw attention to selected states, important objects, visual markers, and short emphasis lines.
- Prefer dark blue text or surfaces over black when a branded neutral is needed.
- Do not let generated documents or web pages fall back to the old prototype blue/cyan palette.
- Keep Study Buddy marks visible but restrained: the identity should feel integrated into the tool, not like a splash screen pasted onto it.
