# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Study Buddy contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, welcoming,
and harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while
responding to impact, respect privacy, and make space for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications,
or sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Report ordinary conduct concerns to the maintainer through the least public
available GitHub channel. If confidentiality is necessary and no suitable
private contact is available, use the repository's private vulnerability report
form and mark the report as a conduct concern. Do not include unrelated secrets.

The maintainer will acknowledge a report when practical, review conflicts of
interest, and communicate the outcome to the reporter where doing so is safe.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, discussions,
reviews, community events, and public representation of the project.
